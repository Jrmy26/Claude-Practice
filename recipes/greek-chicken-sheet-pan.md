# Greek Lemon Chicken Sheet Pan

A garlic, oregano and smoked paprika marinade seasons lean chicken breast deeply, and a squeeze of lemon and a little Parmesan after roasting keep it bright without any sauce.

**Serves:** 4 | **Prep:** 15 min | **Cook:** 30 min
**Diet:** gluten-free, egg-free
**Contains:** milk (Parmesan)

## Ingredients
### Main
- 2 lb (900 g) boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 14 oz (400 g) broccoli, cut into 1 1/2-inch (4 cm) florets
- 2 red capsicums (300 g), cut into 1-inch (2.5 cm) pieces
- 1 medium red onion (150 g), cut into 1-inch (2.5 cm) wedges
- 2 cups (300 g) cherry tomatoes

### Marinade
- 1 Tbsp (15 mL) extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp (2 g) finely grated lemon zest
- 4 garlic cloves (12 g), grated
- 1 Tbsp (3 g) dried oregano
- 1 tsp (2 g) smoked paprika
- 1 tsp (6 g) table salt
- 1/2 tsp (1 g) ground black pepper

### Cooking
- 1 Tbsp (15 mL) extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/4 tsp (1.5 g) table salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5 g) ground black pepper

### To finish
- 2 Tbsp (30 mL) lemon juice
- 1/3 cup (30 g) grated Parmesan

## Equipment
- 2 rimmed baking trays, about 18 x 13 inches (46 x 33 cm) or the largest your oven fits, lined with foil
- Instant-read thermometer
- 4 meal prep containers

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 450°F (230°C) with racks in the upper-middle and lower-middle positions, about 15 minutes, until fully preheated, and line both trays with foil.
2. Cut the chicken into 1 1/2-inch (4 cm) chunks, keeping the pieces close in size so they finish cooking together.
3. Toss the chicken with all the marinade ingredients for 30 seconds, until evenly coated.
4. Refrigerate the chicken for at least 20 minutes, or up to 24 hours; the salt seasons the meat deeply and helps it hold its juices, and the lemon juice waits until the end because acid turns chicken mealy in a long marinade.
5. Toss the broccoli, capsicums and onion with the cooking oil, salt and pepper on one tray for 30 seconds, then spread them in a single layer.
6. Roast the vegetables on the lower-middle rack for 10 minutes, until the broccoli edges start to brown.
7. Spread the chicken on the second tray with space between the pieces, so it browns instead of steaming.
8. Scatter the cherry tomatoes over the vegetables, then roast the chicken on the upper-middle rack, with the vegetables below it, for 14 to 18 minutes, until the chicken is golden at the edges, the thickest piece reaches 165°F (74°C), and the vegetables are tender and browned in spots.
9. Drizzle the lemon juice over the hot chicken and vegetables and let them rest on the trays for 5 minutes, until the juices settle back into the meat.
10. Pack the chicken and vegetables into the 4 containers and sprinkle each with the Parmesan.

## Substitutions
- Smoked paprika: regular ground paprika; the chicken tastes less smoky.
- Red capsicums: green or yellow capsicums; green is slightly more bitter.
- Parmesan (for a dairy allergy): leave it out and add an extra pinch of salt with the lemon juice; it saves about 30 kcal per serving.
- Oven too small for 2 trays: roast the vegetables first for 20 to 25 minutes, then the chicken for 14 to 18 minutes on the same tray, and add the tomatoes for the last 10 minutes of the vegetables.
- Adding a base: this version has no grain, to stay under 500 kcal. Quinoa, 30 g uncooked per serving (about 1/2 cup cooked), adds about 110 kcal and 4 g protein, bringing the total to about 570 kcal, over the limit. To stay under 500 kcal, use 10 g uncooked per serving (about 2 Tbsp cooked), which brings it to about 497 kcal.

## Storage
Refrigerate within 2 hours for up to 4 days, or freeze up to 2 months (the vegetables will soften). Reheat in the microwave, loosely covered, for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring halfway, until steaming and 165°F (74°C).
